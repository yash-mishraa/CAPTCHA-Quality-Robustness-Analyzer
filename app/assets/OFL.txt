Bundled interface fonts
=======================

This dashboard bundles three open-license typefaces so that the local app does
not call a font CDN at runtime:

- Source Serif 4 (display and headings)
- IBM Plex Sans (body and controls)
- IBM Plex Mono (metrics, identifiers, and technical metadata)

Each font is distributed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. The full
license texts are included next to the font files:

- fonts/OFL-SourceSerif4.txt
- fonts/OFL-IBMPlexSans.txt
- fonts/OFL-IBMPlexMono.txt

Only Latin-subset WOFF2 files are included. No font from the ten-typeface
benchmark roster is bundled, and no benchmark font binary is redistributed.
